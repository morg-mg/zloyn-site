Privacy Policy — ZloyN

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

ZloyN ("we", "our", or "the Application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you connect your TikTok account to ZloyN.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize ZloyN via TikTok Login Kit, we receive only the minimum data permitted by the approved scopes:

  • Basic account identifier (open_id) and display status (via user.info.basic);
  • OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens required to upload videos on your behalf (via video.publish).

3. How We Use Information

Collected data and authorization tokens are used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok profile inside the Application;
  • Upload and publish user-approved short-form video clips to your TikTok account.

4. Data Storage & Security

OAuth tokens are stored securely on an encrypted, access-restricted private server (chmod 600) and are never shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your authorization tokens only while your account remains connected. You can revoke ZloyN's access at any time in your TikTok app settings (Manage app permissions) or request immediate deletion of all stored tokens by emailing workacconeru@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
